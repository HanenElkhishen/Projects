# Economic Analysis of Egypt's GDP (2014–2024) 🇪🇬📈

A comprehensive data analysis project examining Egypt's **Gross Domestic Product (GDP)** and **GDP Growth Rate** over the past decade (2014-2024). This project explores how macroeconomic policy decisions and global events—including currency devaluations, the COVID-19 pandemic, and geopolitical conflicts—have fundamentally shaped the Egyptian economy.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This analysis goes beyond the numbers to uncover the narrative of Egypt's economic journey. GDP represents more than production metrics; it reflects currency dynamics, economic resilience, and a nation's capacity to navigate global disruptions.

By integrating global economic trends with domestic performance indicators, this project explains the drivers behind GDP fluctuations, particularly when measured in US Dollars, providing context for seemingly counterintuitive trends.

### **Research Questions**
- How have global shocks and domestic policy reforms influenced Egypt's GDP trajectory?
- What explains the disconnect between nominal GDP values and growth rates?
- How has currency devaluation masked underlying economic productivity?

## 📂 Project Structure & Analysis

### 1️⃣ **Egypt GDP Analysis** (`Egypt GDP.ipynb`)

This notebook analyzes the **Total GDP value in USD**, deconstructing the economic timeline into three distinct phases.

#### **Methodology:**
- Comprehensive data cleaning and preprocessing using Python
- Implementation of zero-baseline visualization techniques for accurate trend representation
- Currency-adjusted analysis to distinguish between nominal and real economic changes
- Annotation of major economic events and policy interventions

#### **Key Findings:**

**Phase 1: Stability and Shock (2014-2017)**
- Initial GDP: **$305.6B** (2014)
- Impact of global oil price collapse (2014-2016) on regional investment flows
- November 2016 currency flotation resulted in GDP dropping to **$248.4B** (2017)
- *Critical insight:* This decline reflected exchange rate adjustment, not productive capacity loss

**Phase 2: Resilience and Recovery (2018-2021)**
- Sustained growth trajectory despite global pandemic
- GDP reached **$383.8B** by 2020
- Growth drivers: infrastructure megaprojects, structural reforms, and diversification efforts
- Egypt was one of few economies to maintain positive growth during COVID-19

**Phase 3: Peak and Pressure (2022-2024)**
- Historic peak of **$476.7B** achieved in 2022
- Subsequent adjustment to **$389.1B** by 2024 due to:
  - Global inflationary pressures
  - Russia-Ukraine conflict spillover effects
  - Further exchange rate realignments
  - External financing constraints

---

### 2️⃣ **GDP Growth Rate Analysis** (`Egypt GDP Growth Rate%.ipynb`)

This notebook measures the **velocity of economic expansion**, analyzing year-over-year percentage changes to assess economic health independent of currency effects.

#### **Methodology:**
- Calculation of Year-over-Year (YoY) growth rates
- Identification of inflection points and trend reversals
- Correlation analysis between growth patterns and external events
- Decomposition of growth drivers by sector where applicable

#### **Key Findings:**

**The Growth-GDP Paradox:**
- Demonstrates how positive growth rates can coexist with declining USD-denominated GDP
- Local production (measured in EGP) continued expanding while currency valuation changed
- This disconnect highlights the importance of analyzing multiple economic indicators

**Performance Highlights:**
- **2014:** Post-transition contraction at **2.9%**
- **2015-2019:** Steady acceleration from **4.4%** to **5.6%** (pre-COVID peak)
- **2022:** Decade-high growth of **6.6%** driven by:
  - Tourism sector rebound
  - Telecommunications infrastructure expansion
  - Pent-up consumer demand
- **2023-2024:** Moderation to **3.8-4.4%** due to:
  - Record inflation rates
  - Foreign currency liquidity constraints
  - Regional geopolitical tensions affecting Suez Canal revenues
  - Monetary tightening measures

## 📊 Executive Summary

### **Major Conclusions:**

1. **Economic Resilience:** The Egyptian economy demonstrated remarkable adaptability across multiple systemic shocks—currency flotation (2016), global pandemic (2020), and geopolitical conflicts (2022-2024).

2. **Currency Dynamics:** USD-denominated GDP analysis requires careful interpretation. Apparent declines often represent "exchange rate realism" and monetary policy adjustments rather than contractions in actual economic output.

3. **Structural Progress:** Despite volatility, the economic baseline in 2024 significantly exceeds 2014 levels, with improved fundamentals including:
   - Diversified revenue streams
   - Enhanced infrastructure
   - Strengthened institutional frameworks
   - Expanded productive capacity

4. **Growth Trajectory:** While growth has moderated from 2022 peaks, the economy maintains positive momentum with potential for acceleration as external pressures ease.
