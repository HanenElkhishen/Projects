# Economic Analysis of Egypt’s GDP (2014–2024) 🇪🇬📈

A comprehensive data analysis project examining Egypt's **Gross Domestic Product (GDP)** and **GDP Growth Rate** over the last decade (2014-2024). This project explores how local economic decisions and global events—such as currency devaluations, the COVID-19 pandemic, and geopolitical conflicts—have shaped the Egyptian economy.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
The goal of this analysis is to interpret the "story" behind the numbers. GDP isn't just a measure of production; it’s a reflection of real economic activity, currency strength, and a nation's ability to absorb global shocks. 

In this project, I linked global economic trends with local performance to explain why the numbers shifted, especially when measured in US Dollars.

## 📂 Project Structure & Insights

### 1️⃣ Egypt GDP Analysis (`Egypt GDP.ipynb`)
This notebook focuses on the **Total GDP value** in USD. The analysis breaks down the timeline into three major phases:

**What was done:**
* Cleaned and reshaped the dataset using Python.
* Created a professional "Zero-Baseline" Line Chart to show the true economic floor.
* Analyzed the impact of currency valuation on the total GDP figure.

**Key Insights:**
* **Phase 1 (2014-2017):** Initially stable, but hit by the global oil price crash (2014-2016) which affected regional investments. In 2017, the GDP dropped to **$248.4B** due to the currency flotation (EGP devaluation), not a drop in production.
* **Phase 2 (2018-2021):** A period of resilience. Despite the COVID-19 pandemic, the economy continued to grow, reaching **$383.8B**, supported by infrastructure investments and earlier reforms.
* **Phase 3 (2022-2024):** Egypt reached an all-time high of **$476.7B** in 2022. However, global inflation and the Russia-Ukraine war led to new exchange rate adjustments, bringing the dollar value to **$389.1B** by 2024.

---

### 2️⃣ GDP Growth Rate Analysis (`Egypt GDP Growth Rate%.ipynb`)
This notebook measures the **speed and health** of the economy, looking at the percentage of growth year-over-year.

**What was done:**
* Calculated the Year-over-Year (YoY) percentage changes.
* Visualized the growth fluctuations to identify peaks and slowdowns.
* Correlated growth dips with specific global crises.

**Key Insights:**
* **The Disconnect:** This analysis shows that the "Growth Rate" can remain positive even if the "GDP in USD" drops. This is because the local production (EGP) is still moving forward while the currency valuation (USD) changes.
* **The 2022 Peak:** Growth reached **6.6%** in 2022, the highest in the decade, driven by a post-COVID rebound and tourism recovery.
* **2023-2024 Slowdown:** Growth slowed to **2.4%** due to record-high inflation, foreign currency shortages, and regional geopolitical tensions impacting revenues like the Suez Canal.

---

## 📊 Summary of Findings
1. **Resilience against Shocks:** The Egyptian economy proved its ability to survive multiple crises (Flotation, Pandemic, Wars, and Monetary Tightening).
2. **Currency Factor:** Any reading of Egypt's GDP in Dollars must consider the exchange rate context. A dip in the chart often reflects "Exchange Rate Realism" rather than a stop in local production.
3. **Growth Potential:** Despite the challenges, the economic floor today is significantly higher than it was in 2014, with a positive outlook for future expansion.
